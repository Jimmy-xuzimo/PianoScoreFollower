智能琴谱（PianoScoreFollower）隐私权政策

适用于 Android 应用「智能琴谱」，应用包名 com.pianoscorefollower.app。

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日　·　最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

一句话说明：「智能琴谱」是一款完全在本机运行的应用。它不收集、不存储、不传输您的任何个人信息，也没有联网权限。麦克风仅用于在您的设备上实时识别琴音，音频不会被录制或发送到任何地方。

1.我们收集与处理的信息

「智能琴谱」不要求注册或登录账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、通讯录、位置、设备标识符或广告标识符等任何可识别个人身份的信息。以下说明应用在运行时会接触到的数据及其处理方式：

数据类型用途处理方式
麦克风音频（敏感数据） 在您演奏时实时识别音高，用于跟谱、自动翻页与音准校准。 仅在设备内存中实时处理，不录音、不保存、不上传；分析结束后立即丢弃。
您导入的乐谱文件（PDF、图片、MIDI、MusicXML） 用于显示、滚动谱面与播放。 仅保存到应用在本机的私有存储目录，不会上传；您可随时在应用内删除。
应用设置（深浅模式、界面配色等） 记住您的偏好，下次打开时保持一致。 仅保存在本机，不会上传。

我们不会收集的信息

  • 个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话、账号）
  • 位置信息
  • 通讯录、短信、通话记录
  • 照片与视频（您主动导入的乐谱图片除外，且仅保存在本机）
  • 设备标识符、广告标识符、使用行为与崩溃日志

2.信息的存储与保留

  • 上述所有数据均保存在您的设备本地，应用没有向任何服务器发送数据的能力。
  • 导入的乐谱与页面索引保存在应用私有目录中，您可以随时在应用内删除；卸载应用会一并清除这些数据。
  • 关于系统备份：如果您的 Android 设备启用了系统级的「备份到 Google 云端」功能，操作系统可能会将应用数据纳入备份。这属于 Android 系统与您 Google 账号的功能，并非由本应用发起或控制。您可以在系统设置中关闭该功能。

3.信息的共享与第三方

  • 我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租或共享您的数据。
  • 应用未集成任何广告、统计分析、用户行为追踪或崩溃上报类第三方 SDK。
  • 应用未申请网络访问权限（INTERNET），因此从技术上无法将您的数据发送到设备之外。应用内的乐谱排版与音源均在本地资源中加载，不访问互联网。

4.应用申请的权限

  • RECORD_AUDIO（麦克风）：用于实时采集您演奏的声音以识别音高。仅在您使用跟谱或音准校准功能时启用；音频不会被录制、保存或上传。您可以随时在系统设置中撤回该权限，撤回后跟谱与校音功能将不可用，其余功能不受影响。

应用不申请存储、位置、相机、通讯录等任何其他敏感权限。

5.儿童隐私

「智能琴谱」是一款面向普通用户的乐谱工具，不以儿童为目标受众，也不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用本身不收集任何个人信息，因此不存在针对儿童的专项数据收集行为。

6.数据安全

由于应用不收集、不传输任何数据，您的信息始终留在自己的设备上，不存在因网络传输或服务器存储而导致的数据泄露风险。请妥善保管您的设备与锁屏密码，以保护设备上的本地数据。

7.您的权利

  • 访问与删除：您可以在应用内查看并删除已导入的乐谱与页面；卸载应用即可清除全部本地数据。
  • 撤回权限：您可以在「系统设置 → 应用 → 智能琴谱 → 权限」中随时关闭麦克风权限。
  • 由于我们并不持有您的任何个人数据，因此无需向任何机构提出数据访问或删除请求。

8.本政策的变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面顶部的「最后更新」日期，并在本页公布最新版本。重大变更时，我们会通过应用更新说明等方式另行提示。继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy — PianoScoreFollower (智能琴谱)

For the Android application “智能琴谱”, package name com.pianoscorefollower.app.

Effective date: October 7, 2026　·　Last updated: October 7, 2026

In short: PianoScoreFollower runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information and has no network permission. The microphone is used only to recognize piano notes in real time on your device — audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.

1.Information We Collect and Process

The app requires no account or login and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. The following describes the data the app may touch while running:

Data typePurposeHow it is handled
Microphone audio (sensitive data) Real-time pitch recognition while you play, used for score following, automatic page turning, and tuning. Processed in device memory only. Not recorded, not stored, not uploaded; discarded immediately after analysis.
Sheet-music files you import (PDF, images, MIDI, MusicXML) To display and scroll the score, and to play it back. Stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Never uploaded. You can delete them anytime in the app.
App settings (theme mode, color palette) To remember your preferences between sessions. Stored locally on your device only. Never uploaded.

What we do NOT collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, messages, or call logs
  • Photos and videos (except sheet-music images you deliberately import, which stay on your device)
  • Device or advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash logs

2.Storage and Retention

  • All data described above is kept locally on your device. The app has no ability to send data to any server.
  • Imported scores and page indexes are stored in the app's private directory and can be deleted in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data.
  • System backup: if your Android device has “Back up to Google Drive” enabled, the operating system may include app data in that backup. This is a feature of Android and your Google account, not something the app initiates or controls. You can disable it in your system settings.

3.Sharing and Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app does not request the network permission (INTERNET), so it is technically incapable of transmitting your data off the device. Score rendering and the instrument sound source are loaded from local resources, without accessing the internet.

4.Permissions We Request

  • RECORD_AUDIO (microphone): used to capture your playing in real time for pitch recognition. It is active only while you use score following or tuning; audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded. You may revoke it at any time in your system settings — score following and tuning will stop working, and all other features remain available.

The app does not request storage, location, camera, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions.

5.Children's Privacy

PianoScoreFollower is a sheet-music tool for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is no child-specific data collection.

6.Data Security

Because the app neither collects nor transmits data, your information stays on your own device and is not exposed to network-transmission or server-storage risks. Please protect your device with a lock screen to safeguard local data.

7.Your Rights

  • Access and deletion: you can view and delete imported scores in the app; uninstalling the app clears all local data.
  • Revoking permission: you can turn off the microphone permission anytime under Settings → Apps → 智能琴谱 → Permissions.
  • Since we hold none of your personal data, no data-access or deletion request to us is necessary.

8.Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and publish the latest version here. For material changes we will provide additional notice, such as in the app's release notes. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
